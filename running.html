Daily additions — 2026-05-19

  • Adaptive attention/state interfaces need rare-evidence sign-off. GQLA, STS, block attention, and speculative decoding variants all expose state layout or token reachability as a policy/control variable. Experiment: dense vs block vs sparse vs latent/KV-compressed attention under equal latency/KV budget on early rare facts, stale corrections, negative evidence, and distractors; log evidence reachability, output/logit drift, and latency.
  • RLVR progress is shifting toward strategy coverage plus aggregation geometry. GRLO, strategy-guided RLVR, Hölder Policy Optimisation, and MetaAgent-X reinforce that rollout distribution and sequence-to-token credit mapping are the real objects. Experiment: fixed rollout set comparing exploration nudges, aggregation rules, dense teachers, and workflow-level rewards; measure prefix-local KL, changed-token sparsity, strategy diversity, and gradient-cosine.
  • Speech-factor survival should now include speaker verification, impaired speech, and streaming timing. SpeakerLLM, streaming SpeechLLM translation, oral-cancer ASR, and ASR-as-enhancement-evaluator critiques show that transcript/WER metrics can hide factor failures. Experiment: raw audio vs transcript vs corrected transcript vs codec/encoder states with speaker/accent/impairment/phoneme/prosody/latency probes and shuffled-metadata controls.
  • Interpretability units need equivalence and intervention prediction tests. Tensor similarity and Exemplar Partitioning add cheaper or more formal unit-comparison tools, while SAE explanation-collision work warns against readability-only validation. Experiment: compare SAE/EP/tensor/path units by held-out patch-effect prediction, ablation prediction, cross-checkpoint equivalence, and side-effect specificity.
  • Local runtime paths are now cheap enough to audit as causal transformations. ExecuTorch MLX plus speculative latency/window papers and quantization-permanent unlearning make PyTorch/MLX/low-bit/speculative paths a practical intervention-survival testbed. Experiment: one small model across runtime paths with rare facts, long context, refusal/edit behavior, latency, memory, and output/logit drift.

Weak signals / bottlenecks

  • No single dominant pattern today; durable memory splits across adaptive attention/state reachability, RL rollout/credit geometry, speech factor localization, causal interpretability scorecards, and runtime-path drift.
  • Evaluation audits (geospatial FMs, ProofGrid, Physics-R1, ASR enhancement critique) reinforce that mechanism claims collapse without data provenance, process evidence, and metric-slice controls.

Experiment: compare full context, retrieval, learned memory, KV writes/compression, and sparse attention on rare clues, stale corrections, negative evidence, and distractors under equal token/time/KV/memory budgets.

Experiment: fixed math/tool rollout set; compare outcome-only, few-shot-guided with decay, fixed-teacher, and self-distilled/co-evolving guidance; log rollout diversity, teacher disagreement, changed-token sparsity, and gradient-cosine.

Experiment: small MoE with rare/domain factors; compare fixed top-k vs binary masks; measure router entropy, active experts, expert ablation specificity, rare-feature retention, and latency.

From Text to Talk, SpeakerLLM, audio tool-calling evaluation, and fine-grained speech understanding suggest voice-agent failures need attribution to acoustic perception, speaker evidence, transcript content, prosody/timing, language reasoning, or tool schema execution.

  • Quantization remains a hard sign-off requirement for interventions: outlier-injection and quantization-permanent unlearning show low-bit deployment can invert, erase, or preserve targeted edits depending on mechanism.

Weekly synthesis — 2026-05-09

Durable belief updates

  • Promote rollout-lifecycle diagnostics for reasoning/tool RL. The repeated signal from GFCR, PORTool, Reinforced Agent, vLLM correctness, StraTA, A^2TGPO, LoPE, and SIOP is that the optimizer sees a trajectory distribution, not a benchmark. Treat generation, filtering, control, replay, and runtime as parts of the objective. Minimal next step: toy math/tool environment with controlled lifecycle perturbations and gradient-cosine proxy against a wide/clean sampler.
  • Promote mechanism-preserving inference and context-budget harnesses. KV cache, speculation, cache handoff, expert prefetch, serving backend, and long-context access are experimental conditions. Measure output/logit/activation drift, rare-feature failures, rollout reproducibility, cache bytes, latency, bandwidth, and evidence retention per cost. Fold “useful KV bytes,” “KV handoff,” “serving perturbations,” and “runtime correctness” into this umbrella.
  • Test shared MoE expert pools as causal modular capacity. UniPool and EMO move MoE from sparse-compute framing to allocation/gradient-sharing geometry. Tiny layer-local vs global-pool MoE experiment should measure usage matrices $U_{\ell,g}$, router entropy, $I(G;F\mid\ell)$, expert ablation losses $\Delta_{g,F}$, cross-layer gradient cosine, overflow/drop rate, and rare-feature retention.
  • Promote speech factor survival probes. Audio evidence converged on factor entanglement: speaker/script/accent leakage, phoneme/content, prosody, rare entities, context metadata, codec intelligibility, and long-form/full-duplex timing. Merge codec-token, speaker leakage, entity-dense ASR, and speech-context questions into one probing harness.
  • Watch intervention auditing as a future harness. Prompt-local steering, conceptors, automatic side-effect discovery, reward-model misalignment, safety drift, warmth/sycophancy, and preference geometry all point to intervention side-effect audits, but the local setup still needs narrowing.
  • Watch visual/nameability bottlenecks. Persistent visual memory, TC-JEPA, GenLIP, end-to-end semantic tokenizers, MMDG-Bench, and diffusion outlier tokens suggest multimodal factor tests: visual persistence over generation length and nameable vs non-nameable factor retention.

Recurring bottlenecks

  • Gradient allocation: routers, quantizers, preference objectives, and data selection decide which weak/rare signals receive usable updates.
  • State accessibility: KV cache, long context, procedural registers, agent memory, visual memory, and speech latents are only useful if accessible at the relevant decision point.
  • Trajectory distribution: RL/tool-agent performance depends on what trajectories are generated, filtered, controlled, replayed, and served correctly.
  • Factor entanglement: speech/audio/vision/style/safety signals repeatedly collapse independent factors into one score or representation.
  • Aggregate metric camouflage: perplexity, WER, final-answer reward, tokens/sec, compile success, safety pass rate, and broad accuracy hide slice-level mechanism failures.
  • Cost normalization: long context, inference acceleration, agent context operations, repeated inference, and kernel generation need equal token/time/tool/memory/bandwidth budgets.

Missing tools / annoying manual things

  • Unified runtime-perturbation logger for small models: prompt matrix in, serving paths out, output/logit/activation drift, latency/memory/cache/bandwidth, rare-slice errors.
  • Rollout-lifecycle logger for toy agents: trajectories, tools, verifier labels, branch/stop decisions, replay age, runtime backend, and update-direction proxy.
  • MoE routing probe toolkit: $U_{\ell,g}$ heatmaps, factor MI, causal expert ablations, cross-layer gradient cosine, rare-feature overflow/drop reports.
  • Speech factor probe boilerplate: manifest labels for speaker/script/accent/phoneme/prosody/entity/context, extractor wrappers, probes, gradient reversal, EHR/WER/prosody metrics.
  • Cost-normalized corpus interface benchmark: dense retrieval, raw grep/read access, signatures/summaries, context operations, and full context under one budget API.
  • Intervention side-effect audit template: before/after contrastive generations, natural-language side-effect hypotheses, and validation slices.
  • Kernel repair audit harness: compile/correctness/speed/bandwidth logged at every repair step.

Cheap-paper opportunities

  • Rollout lifecycle lab: small deterministic math/tool sandbox; perturb lifecycle stage; log gradient-cosine proxy and failure localization.
  • Shared-pool MoE probe: tiny synthetic-factor MoE; compare layer-local vs global-pool experts under matched active compute.
  • Mechanism-preserving inference harness: one small model, two runtime paths, rare-feature/long-context prompts, drift-vs-speed plots.
  • Speech factor survival notebook: public WavLM/ECAPA/codec states, factor probes, shuffled metadata, gradient-reversal nuisance removal.
  • Long-context access benchmark: local corpus with exact constraints and distractors; compare retrieval/signature/raw-tool/full-context interfaces under equal budget.
  • KernelBench-X mini reproduction: small Triton task suite with repair-loop correctness-speed tradeoff logging.

Weekly synthesis — 2026-05-16

Durable belief updates

  • Promote sparse branch/update geometry for reasoning RL. The week’s RL evidence points to localized probability-mass movement over fixed rollout candidates: graph/listwise preference targets, entropy-gated corrections, OPD baselines, rubric decomposition, old-logit fidelity, and dense token teachers. Minimal next step: fixed math/code/tool rollout set; compare objective-induced prefix-local KL, promoted-token base rank, changed-token sparsity, teacher staleness, and gradient-cosine against an on-policy reference.
  • Promote state-interface benchmarks beyond generic long context. Test them under equal token/time/KV/memory budgets on sparse clues, negative evidence, stale corrections, and rare early facts.
  • Promote deployment-path validity for interventions. Quantization-permanent unlearning and quantization-conditioned attacks show low-bit deployment can erase, alias, or invert small edits. Any unlearning/refusal/LoRA/steering claim should be evaluated fp16 vs NF4/GPTQ/AWQ/GGUF-style paths with update-to-bin ratios, logit/activation drift, and behavior survival.
  • Test temporary-lossy pretraining as optimization-path engineering. Lighthouse Attention and Token-Superposition Training suggest coarse training paths can accelerate broad competence, but only if rare/order-sensitive features recover. Measure per-slice gradient-energy retention and recovery lag under matched FLOPs.
  • Promote speech-factor survival sign-off with timing/provenance labels. The audio harness should now include emission timing, recording-condition evidence, transcript disagreement, and speaker-evidence reasoning, not only phoneme/speaker/prosody/entity factors.
  • Watch non-AR/byte/diffusion/flow diagnostics. Fast BLT, DLM layer collapse, STARFlow2, DiffRetriever, and Mean Mode Screaming warn against importing AR/KV/entropy intuitions directly. Promote only after runnable small artifacts make controlled comparison cheap.
  • Evaluate interpretability/data-selection structure causally. Shared MoE pools, SoftSAE/NLA, Jet/path decompositions, and neuron-aware selection should be judged by held-out patch/ablation/interference prediction, not readability or reconstruction alone.

Recurring bottlenecks

  • State placement: context, KV, learned memory, retrieval, parallel streams, session state, speech timing state, and rollout traces preserve different information and fail differently.
  • Credit-channel width: final-answer rewards, graph/listwise labels, rubric components, internal values, token-level teachers, and verifiers create different gradients from the same behavior.
  • Deployment aliasing: quantization and optimized kernels can turn a validated intervention into a different causal object.
  • Rare-evidence eviction: adaptive controllers, KV retention, memory compression, signatures, and audio compression all tend to lose weak but decisive factors.
  • Stale supervision: off-policy old logits, fixed teachers, replay buffers, and persistent memories can be precise but semantically stale.
  • Aggregate metric camouflage: WER, final-answer reward, tokens/sec, compile success, perplexity, and broad safety pass rates remain insufficient.

  • Deployment intervention survival harness: edit/LoRA/steer/unlearn, quantize through several paths, report update-to-bin ratios, drift, behavior survival, latency, and memory.
  • State-interface benchmark runner: full context vs retrieval vs signatures/summaries vs learned memory vs KV retention vs persistent sessions under one budget API.
  • Speech-factor manifest with timing, recording condition, transcript disagreement, speaker/accent/prosody/entity/context labels.
  • Causal interpretability scorecard: reconstruction/readability plus held-out patch-effect prediction, ablation prediction, and intervention transfer.

  • MoE/global-pool causal capacity probe: synthetic factors, expert ablations, router entropy, rare-feature retention.
