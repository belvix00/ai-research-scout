Daily additions — 2026-05-04

  • Gradient-channel bottlenecks as measurable small-model pathology — General theme: some training signals get compressed, diluted, or routed through narrow interfaces before they become robust behavior. Experiment: train tiny models on controlled skewed data and log per-feature gradient norms, cosine drift, and downstream accuracy under several auxiliary-objective or adapter variants. Keep this broad; do not force new ideas through a single prior hypothesis.
  • KV-cache/quantization stress harness for mechanism experiments on-device — Evidence-backed opportunity: inference kernels are improving, but mechanism papers rarely report how cache quantization/speculation changes hidden-state trajectories, not just tokens/sec. Experiment: Run the same small model under fp16, low-bit weights, low-bit KV, and speculative decoding; compare activation cosine drift, attention entropy, failure cases, and SAE feature firing stability. Evidence: Evidence: SpeCache: Speculative Key-Value Caching for Efficient Generation of LLMs and recent configured repo/HF activity around inference systems.
  • Codec-token speech models: where do linguistic vs speaker features separate? — Speculation: codec language models may have a sharper layerwise split between phonetic content, prosody, and speaker identity than text LMs have for syntax/semantics. Experiment: Use a public speech representation/model checkpoint; fit linear probes and small SAEs on codec-token hidden states for phoneme, speaker, and prosody labels on a tiny dataset. Evidence: Evidence: Qwen3-TTS Technical Report from audio/speech-focused sources.

Daily additions — 2026-05-04

  • Treat multimodal tokenizers as measurable gradient gates — GenLIP and end-to-end 1D semantic tokenizer work suggest the perception→sequence interface is now directly supervised by generative losses. Experiment: controlled synthetic visual factors, compare frozen tokenizer vs jointly trained tokenizer vs language-token-supervised encoder; measure factor probes and generation errors by rarity/nameability.
  • Build an audio identity-leakage probe for codec and speaker representations — LASE shows speaker encoders can leak script/language information with accent-conditional failures; Talker-T2AV supports separating high-level cross-modal semantics from low-level rendering. Experiment: WavLM/ECAPA/codec probes for speaker, language/script, phoneme, and prosody, with optional gradient-reversal nuisance removal.
  • Use MoE routing masks as a mechanistic intervention, not just a safety adapter — MASCing and Laguna-style router designs make expert routing a controllable mechanism, not just an efficiency detail. Experiment: toy MoE on separable domains/rare features; intervene with route masks or router biases and measure causal effects on rare-feature behavior.
  • Convert deployment/inference feedback into a small agent-learning experiment — Learning While Deploying and Apple Reinforced Agent show correction signals moving into deployment/inference loops. Experiment: tool-calling sandbox with reviewer that can block/repair calls, plus structured skill interfaces, to test whether feedback becomes reusable memory.
  • Measure serving-stack choices as perturbations to model mechanisms — MLX/vLLM/SGLang activity around quantized matmuls, CUDA graphs, MoE serving, and prefill/decode layout suggests serving choices are experimental perturbations. Experiment: compare logits/activations/failures under baseline vs optimized low-bit/graph/cache paths on rare-fact and long-context prompts.

  • Interfaces are the recurring bottleneck today: tokenizers, speaker encoders, MoE routers, skill schemas, and serving caches decide what information survives between subsystems.
  • Inference systems should be logged as mechanism perturbations, not just throughput infrastructure.
