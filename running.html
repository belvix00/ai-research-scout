Daily additions — 2026-05-04

  • Gradient-channel bottlenecks as measurable small-model pathology — General theme: some training signals get compressed, diluted, or routed through narrow interfaces before they become robust behavior. Experiment: train tiny models on controlled skewed data and log per-feature gradient norms, cosine drift, and downstream accuracy under several auxiliary-objective or adapter variants. Keep this broad; do not force new ideas through a single prior hypothesis.
  • KV-cache/quantization stress harness for mechanism experiments on-device — Evidence-backed opportunity: inference kernels are improving, but mechanism papers rarely report how cache quantization/speculation changes hidden-state trajectories, not just tokens/sec. Experiment: Run the same small model under fp16, low-bit weights, low-bit KV, and speculative decoding; compare activation cosine drift, attention entropy, failure cases, and SAE feature firing stability. Evidence: Evidence: SpeCache: Speculative Key-Value Caching for Efficient Generation of LLMs and recent configured repo/HF activity around inference systems.
  • Codec-token speech models: where do linguistic vs speaker features separate? — Speculation: codec language models may have a sharper layerwise split between phonetic content, prosody, and speaker identity than text LMs have for syntax/semantics. Experiment: Use a public speech representation/model checkpoint; fit linear probes and small SAEs on codec-token hidden states for phoneme, speaker, and prosody labels on a tiny dataset. Evidence: Evidence: Qwen3-TTS Technical Report from audio/speech-focused sources.

Daily additions — 2026-05-04 test briefing

  • Speech tokenizer as audio interface bottleneck — VibeVoice-like continuous speech tokenizers suggest that rare speakers/accents/phonetic factors may be compressed into weakly separated latent directions. Experiment: measure factor frequency vs codec reconstruction/prediction error on a small speech set.
  • Useful information per KV-cache byte — PagedAttention/vLLM/SGLang/llama.cpp signals suggest agent memory and long-context research should price hidden-state memory, not just tokens. Experiment: synthetic context utility vs KV-cache cost; artifact: “Context Is Not Free.”
  • Low-bit weights as weak-feature gradient starvation — Ternary quantization may disproportionately trap weak gradients from low-frequency or weakly supervised features. Experiment: tiny skewed-data LM with full precision vs ternary vs ternary+adapter side-channel.
  • Procedural reasoning as lost registers — Long step-following failures can be reframed as distributed register-state decay. Experiment: synthetic programs with varying look-back; compare next-token-only vs auxiliary state/MTP-style losses.
  • Visual signal dilution in LVLMs — Long multimodal generation may drift because visual tokens lose attention mass to accumulated text. Experiment: detail-recall accuracy vs generation length, with optional visual-memory refresh baseline.
