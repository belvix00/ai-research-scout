AI Research Scout Resources — 2026-05-04

Plain list of resources collected today. This is intentionally not a synthesis: just source, link, and a short 3-4 line summary so Aman can scan the raw material directly.

Hugging Face Papers/arXiv — VibeVoice Technical Report

Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19205v1

Summary: Audio ML paper about long-form multi-speaker speech synthesis. The key signal is next-token diffusion over continuous speech latents and a continuous speech tokenizer claiming much stronger compression than Encodec-like codecs. Worth scanning for audio tokenizer/interface bottlenecks and codec latent probing ideas.

Hugging Face Papers/arXiv — Efficient Memory Management for Large Language Model Serving with PagedAttention

Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.06180v1

Summary: Inference systems paper around KV-cache memory management and fragmentation. The virtual-memory analogy is useful beyond systems throughput: it frames KV cache as a constrained hidden-state storage budget. Good source for experiments on useful information per cache byte and on-device inference stress tests.

Hugging Face Papers/arXiv — Tequila: Trapping-free Ternary Quantization for Large Language Models

Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23809v2

Summary: Quantization paper focused on ternary weights and avoiding optimization traps. Useful as a mechanism source, not just a compression result. The research angle is whether low-bit constraints selectively damage weak or low-frequency features and how adapter/side-channel paths change that.

arXiv cs.CL — When LLMs Stop Following Steps: A Diagnostic Study of Procedural Execution in Language Models

Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00817v1

Summary: Diagnostic paper on procedural execution failures as sequence length/look-back grows. Useful for thinking about procedural reasoning as distributed state/register decay. Could inspire small synthetic tasks comparing next-token-only training against auxiliary state or multi-step prediction losses.

arXiv cs.CV — Persistent Visual Memory: Sustaining Perception for Deep Generation in LVLMs

Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00814v1

Summary: Multimodal mechanism paper about visual signal dilution during long generation. The useful signal is architectural: keep a persistent visual branch/memory so text tokens do not dominate attention mass over time. Good candidate for a first-principles post or small demo measuring visual recall vs generation length.

Hacker News — The Hidden Costs of Great Abstractions

Link: https://jdgr.net/the-hidden-costs-of-great-abstractions

Summary: General systems weak signal about abstractions hiding real costs. Not an ML paper, but useful as an infra lens: agent frameworks, inference stacks, and training abstractions often hide bottlenecks until debugging or scaling. Keep as background for missing-tool/bottleneck-dashboard ideas.

Hacker News — Using underdrawings for accurate text and numbers

Link: https://samcollins.blog/underdrawings/

Summary: Weak signal around generation with explicit scaffolds/underdrawings. The ML analogy is intermediate plans or constraints that make generation more reliable. Could connect to structured latent planning, visual generation scaffolds, or tool-agent intermediate state.

Apple ML — Reinforced Agent: Inference-Time Feedback for Tool-Calling Agents

Link: https://machinelearning.apple.com/research/reinforced-agent-inference-feedback

Summary: Apple ML post about inference-time feedback for tool-calling agents. Relevant for agent control and memory because it moves some correction/adaptation into inference rather than training. Worth watching for small reproductions around tool-use feedback loops and provenance/trust in agent memory.
