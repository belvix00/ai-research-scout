AI Research Scout — Research Questions Inbox

Purpose: durable inbox for questions generated by daily/weekly scout runs. Questions should be mechanism-first and should not be forced through any single prior hypothesis.

Triage labels

  • open — worth tracking but not yet actionable
  • candidate — could become an experiment/blog section
  • experiment — has a concrete minimal test
  • answered — resolved by evidence, implementation, or decision
  • killed — not worth pursuing now

Open questions

  • candidate Can continuous latent language models expose controllable global plan variables more cleanly than autoregressive hidden states?
  • candidate Does text-conditioned visual representation learning preserve non-nameable visual factors, or does it preferentially amplify caption-nameable semantics?
  • candidate Can prompt-induced activation deltas be compressed into sparse token-local steering rules that outperform global steering vectors on behavior and calibration?
  • candidate Do conceptor/subspace steering methods reduce unintended calibration, truthfulness, or style side effects compared with single-vector activation steering?
  • candidate Which parts of safety/style/preference drift are visible as entropy, calibration, refusal, evidence-use, routing, or hidden-state drift before aggregate evals move?
  • candidate Can activation-pattern similarity select instruction-tuning data better than embedding/text similarity while reducing interference between latent skills?
  • candidate How does logical expressiveness, separate from proof depth, change RLVR gradient quality and sample efficiency in reasoning models?
  • candidate Which compression and sparsity rules differ between autoregressive, byte-level, diffusion, and flow language models once tasks and scale are controlled?
  • candidate Can trajectory-quality probes predict when few-step diffusion/flow generation will beat blind distillation under equal forward-pass budgets?
  • candidate When adaptive inference controllers branch, prune, gaze, route experts, drop multimodal tokens, speculate, or switch submodel size, which rare evidence/rare branch/rare factor slices are most likely to be over-pruned?
  • candidate Do parallel-stream language models preserve independent reasoning variables under matched compute, or do they mainly behave like a more expensive scratchpad?
  • candidate Can rewriting-based poisoning defenses remove triggers without erasing rare legitimate dialect, code-switching, or domain-specific evidence?
  • candidate Does tokenizer optimization via global/convex-relaxation-style objectives reduce rare-token gradient starvation beyond what matched-vocab BPE/Unigram baselines achieve?

Candidate experiments

  • experiment Fixed-rollout reasoning-RL credit geometry. On a fixed math/code/tool rollout set, compare winner SFT, pairwise/listwise preference, OPD/control-variate baselines, rubric rewards, dense teacher distillation, PMI/information filters, contrastive evidence filters, token-level credit assignment, vector/diversity objectives, Hölder/p-norm sequence-to-token maps, and clipped/unclipped advantages. Measure prefix-local KL concentration, branch-credit share, promoted-token base rank, changed-token sparsity, candidate-simplex movement, diversity of correct strategies, teacher staleness, and held-out transfer. See [[2026-05-13-sparse-branch-rl]], [[2026-05-19-advantage-aggregation-geometry]], and [[2026-05-23]].
  • experiment Cost-normalized state / long-context evidence-access benchmark. Compare full context, dense retrieval, compact signatures/summaries, raw corpus tool access, learned memory, KV write/retention policies, persistent sessions, sparse/block attention, RoPE/context-extension variants, quantized/compressed KV, and serving/runtime paths on a local corpus with exact constraints, sparse clues, stale corrections, negative evidence, repeated aliases, and position-swapped distractors. Log binding margins, evidence reachability, attention/logit proxies, answer margins, latency, KV bytes, memory, and cost-normalized accuracy. See [[2026-05-16]], [[2026-05-21-rope-positional-aliasing]], and [[2026-05-23-runtime-certified-attention]].
  • experiment Deployment-path validity / runtime certificate harness. Run the same small model/task under baseline and optimized/deployed paths: fp16/bf16, NF4/GPTQ/AWQ/GGUF-style weights, low-bit/adaptive KV, quantized attention, speculative decoding, cache handoff, runtime LoRA, PyTorch, MLX/ExecuTorch, vLLM/WebGPU where available. For interventions, log update-to-bin ratios, deployed-delta fidelity, logit/activation drift, target-margin survival, and behavior specificity. For attention/KV, log empirical or certified error budget $$B$$, decision margin $$M$$, certificate slack $$M-B$$, fallback rate, tail latency, and rare-slice failures. See [[2026-05-17-quantization-intervention-validity]] and [[2026-05-23-runtime-certified-attention]].
  • experiment Speech/audio factor survival probe. Use WavLM/ECAPA/codec states or a small speech-native stack to probe speaker, script/language/accent, impairment, phoneme/content, prosody, entity identity, context metadata, timing/latency, recording condition, transcript-disagreement cues, denoising context, and tool-schema failures. Compare raw audio, transcript-only, corrected transcript, prosody-aware text tokens, codec tokens, denoised audio, stage-wise token masks, and streaming states. Test correct vs shuffled metadata, gradient-reversal nuisance removal, entity-dense synthetic curricula, WER/EHR regression, and real-recording sanity checks. See [[2026-05-16]] and [[2026-05-23]].
  • experiment Causal interpretability scorecard. Compare SAE, SoftSAE/adaptive sparsity, exemplar partitioning, tensor similarity, path/Jets decompositions, natural-language explanations, and sparse multimodal features by reconstruction/readability plus held-out patch-effect prediction, ablation-effect prediction, cross-checkpoint equivalence, side-effect specificity, deployment-path stability, visual-degradation robustness, and data-selection interference reduction.
  • experiment Shared MoE expert pools and route masks as rare-capacity probes. Train tiny layer-local and globally shared expert-pool MoEs on synthetic domain/rare-feature factors. Compare fixed top-k, shared pools, learned binary masks, power/latency-aware schedules, and forced route masks. Measure usage matrices $$U_{\ell,g}$$, router entropy, $$I(G;F\mid\ell)$$, expert ablation losses $$\Delta_{g,F}$$, cross-layer gradient cosine, rare-feature retention, overflow/drop rate, and latency. See [[2026-05-09-moe-expert-allocation]].
  • experiment Temporary-lossy pretraining recovery geometry. Train a tiny LM with an early lossy-token or compressed-attention phase, then restore exact AR/full-attention training. Measure coarse-vs-exact gradient cosine, rare-token/order-sensitive/long-context recovery lag $$\mathrm{Lag}_F$$, per-slice gradient-energy retention $$R_F$$, and matched-FLOP speedup. See [[2026-05-11-temporary-lossy-pretraining]].
  • experiment Tokenizer rare-gradient routing lab. Train tiny LMs on a matched corpus under BPE, Unigram, and a convex-relaxation-inspired/proxy tokenizer at equal vocabulary size. Measure fragmentation, token frequency, gradient frequency, rare-string accuracy, multilingual/code identifier behavior, downstream reasoning-string exact match, compression, and sequence-length confounds.
  • experiment Cheap hallucination/uncertainty probe overlap map. On one small factual QA set, log first-content-token entropy, ordinary logprob, middle-layer attention divergence/KL, semantic self-consistency, answer type, and correctness. Cluster failures by which signal catches them.
  • experiment Visual grounding persistence / attention dilution demo. Create simple images with late-needed details; compare short answer, long answer, and long answer with visual refresh/memory summary. Measure detail accuracy vs generation length and attention-to-image fraction if available.
  • experiment Text-conditioned visual SSL nameability probe. Create/reuse synthetic images with nameable/captioned and non-nameable/uncaptioned factors. Compare visual SSL, text-conditioned SSL, end-to-end tokenizer, and caption-randomized controls with linear probes and local-factor corruption metrics.
