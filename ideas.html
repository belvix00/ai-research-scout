Thesis

Model edits, unlearning, safety interventions, and steering effects should be validated after real deployment transformations such as low-bit quantization, because small updates may not survive binning and outlier-sensitive quantizers can activate new behavior.

Mechanism

Low-bit quantization imposes bin-width thresholds and block/outlier interactions; interventions below those thresholds can vanish or change semantics.

Minimal experiment

Apply a small fact edit/unlearning/refusal intervention to an open LM; evaluate fp16 vs NF4/GPTQ/AWQ/GGUF-style quantized paths; log update-to-bin ratios, output/logit/activation drift, and behavior survival.

Falsifier

Evidence
